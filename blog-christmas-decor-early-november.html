Christmas Decor: Why Early November Is the Right Week

The decor that sells out is never the decor on sale in December. Buy the structural pieces first.

There are two Christmas decor markets. One is the ornament-and-novelty market, which is discounted heavily on December 26th and worth waiting for. The other is the structural market — trees, collars, lighting — which sells out in early December and is never meaningfully discounted while it is still useful.

Early November is the seam between them. Stock is complete, prices have not moved yet, and nothing you buy is competing with the Black Friday electronics rush for your attention or your budget.
