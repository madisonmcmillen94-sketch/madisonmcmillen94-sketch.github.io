The Cozy Decor Starter Kit: Warming Up a Room Before the Holidays

Lighting and texture do most of the work. You do not need a redesign, you need about four things.

Cozy is mostly a lighting problem. A room with one bright overhead fixture will feel cold no matter what you put in it, and a room with three small warm light sources will feel finished even if nothing else changed.

That is genuinely good news, because lamps and candles cost far less than furniture. Here is the short list that does most of the work, and it doubles as your base layer when holiday decor goes up in December.
