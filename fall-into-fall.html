Fall into Fall with The Deal Nest

One season, three acts: autumn at home, Halloween on the porch, and the Christmas decor worth buying before it sells out.

Seasonal shopping goes wrong in the same way every year. Everything gets bought in one panicked stretch in late November, at the exact point in the calendar when prices are highest and stock is thinnest. Spreading the same purchases across three months costs less and takes less out of you.

So this is the season laid out in the order it should be bought, with what actually earns its storage box in each act. Prices were checked against live listings on September 9, 2026 and move around; treat them as the neighborhood, not a promise.
