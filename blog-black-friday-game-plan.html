Black Friday Game Plan 2026: What to Buy vs. What to Skip

Not every 'sale' tag represents a genuine deal. Here's what's actually worth your click this year.

Black Friday offers real savings, but not every “sale” tag represents a genuine deal. Having a strategy before sales launch is the difference between actual value and clever marketing. Here's our breakdown of what's worth your watch list this year, and what you can safely skip.

What to put on your watch list

Robot vacuums. Major floorcare brands traditionally drop to their lowest prices of the year during Black Friday week — look at high-suction models like the eufy super-thin series.

What to skip

Unbranded “off-name” tech bundles. Doorbuster tech from unknown brands built specifically for sale events tends to sacrifice build quality.

Gift cards. Major retailers rarely discount gift cards, and affiliate programs like Amazon Associates exclude gift cards from earning any commission — a plain fact worth knowing whether or not you're the one buying. Stick to physical gift bundles or practical accessories instead.
