Halloween Decor That Earns Its Storage Box

Most Halloween spending is thrown out by November. Here is the part that is not.

Americans spend more on Halloween decor every year than on Halloween candy, and a large share of it goes in the bin on November 1st. Inflatables tear, foam props crack in the attic heat, and the one-year-only novelty piece stops being funny the second time you see it.

The decor that survives is the decor that is really just lighting. It stores flat, it works for more than one holiday, and it does far more to a house at night than any amount of plastic in the yard.
