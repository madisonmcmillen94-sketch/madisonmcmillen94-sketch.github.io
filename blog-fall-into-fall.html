Fall Into Fall: The Autumn Edit That Costs Under $80

Three buys turn a house over for the season. Everything after that is decoration on decoration.

Seasonal decorating gets sold as an all-or-nothing project, and it is not. A room reads as autumn when three things change: what it smells like, what the door looks like, and what the couch feels like. Change those and nobody notices that the rest of the house is the same as it was in July.

That is also the honest budget answer. Under eighty dollars covers all three, and every piece of it comes back out of a box next September, which is the only real test of whether seasonal decor was worth buying in the first place.
