Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The Deal Nest respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain terms, what information we collect and how it's used.

Analytics: Like most websites, we may use basic analytics tools to understand overall traffic patterns (such as which pages are visited and how often). This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify visitors.

Cookies: Some third-party services we link to or embed (such as affiliate networks) may set their own cookies once you click through to their site. We don't control those cookies — check the relevant retailer's privacy policy for details.

Advertising: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Contact form submissions: If you email us or fill out a contact form, we retain that information only to respond to your message. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you have questions about this policy, reach out any time.
