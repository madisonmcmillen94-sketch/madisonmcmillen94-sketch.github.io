After-Christmas Decor Sales: What Is Actually Worth Buying

The week after Christmas is the cheapest decor shopping of the year. Buy the neutrals, skip the novelty.

Holiday decor is one of the few categories with a genuinely reliable price collapse. Retailers do not want to store it for eleven months, so the markdowns that start on December 26 are real rather than theatrical.

The trick is knowing what survives the year in storage and what you will resent having bought. The rule that has never failed me: buy the things that work in November and in February, and skip anything tied to a specific year or trend.
