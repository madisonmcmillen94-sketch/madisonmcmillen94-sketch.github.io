Holiday Kitchen Prep: The Gear to Buy Before the Rush

The appliances you will need in November are cheapest and most available in September.

Every year the same thing happens. Somebody volunteers to host, and three days before the meal they discover the slow cooker died last winter and everything decent is either sold out or marked up.

Kitchen appliances follow a predictable curve: widely available and reasonably priced now, thin on stock and full price by mid-November. Buying the workhorses early is not about being organized, it is about paying less for the same object.
